Observational Evidence from Supernovae for an Accelerating Universe and a Cosmological Constant

Reproducing the data analysis from the High-z Supernova Search Team 1998 paper that provides robust evidence for dark energy and establishes the \( \Lambda \)CDM model as the default cosmological model. MCMC analysis of a simple supernovae likelihood from scratch.

Course

Minicourse on Cosmological Maps and Inference

A minicourse I gave in the CBPF School on Computation and IA, in October 2025. The course consists in analyzing three types of maps: galaxy lensing, galaxy clustering, and CMB temperature. Starting from simulated maps, the course aims to extract the power spectrum using Healpy, provide theoretical predictions using CLASS and use MCMC techniques to extract the values of cosmological parameters.
