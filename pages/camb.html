CAMB Online

The CAMB Einstein-Boltzmann code, compiled from Fortran to WebAssembly and running entirely in your browser. Input the \( \Lambda \)CDM parameter values below and click on the "Add cosmology" button to compute the lensed CMB power spectra, the matter power spectrum at \( z = 0 \) and the distance-redshift relations. Each cosmology joins the list below the button, where you can rename it, choose its color or delete it, and the plots show all of them together. Hover your mouse over the plots to see the values.
